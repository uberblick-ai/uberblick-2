This workspace belongs to the project you created it in: the checkout of the
product these pages are about. You and the agents you connect share the same
pages. It works locally first, syncing through a hub when one is configured.

## What belongs here

- Product intent in your team's own words: what a feature is for and what it must not do.
- Decision records: the question, the answer in force, who decided, and the history of earlier answers.
- Reports whose numbers are records in the page, where people can inspect and comment on them.

These are the pages an agent reads before it builds and keeps true as the product
changes.

## What stays elsewhere

- Tasks and delivery live on GitHub.
- Technical documentation belongs next to the code.
- Meeting notes and company-wide content belong in your team's other tools.

Open **How to Use It** to write your first rule and watch an agent use it.
Replace both starter pages when the workspace becomes yours.
